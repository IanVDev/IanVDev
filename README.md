## AURYA

I'm building [AURYA](https://auryacore.com) as my own product.

AURYA is a platform that turns contracts, approvals and deliveries into verifiable integrity records. With a link and no account, clients and auditors can check those records. Validation gets simpler. Scattered documents and information stop being the source of truth.

It also records what AI agents do on behalf of a company and within which permissions.
